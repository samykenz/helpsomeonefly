In memory

A builder, a mother, and the light of their lives.

in-memory.txt

Adam Robinson built Retention.com and RB2B in Austin and shared everything he learned with other founders. His wife, Helen Sharp, led Influential PR and had just started a ceramics company. Their families remember them as devoted parents, and Emma and Charlie as the light of their lives.

“Helen and Adam were the love affair that will last forever.”From the families' statement

On November 1, 2026, their flight went down near Greenacres, Florida. Nine people were lost. This fund turns every trade into something that keeps others flying.

Adam RobinsonHelen SharpEmma, 4Charlie, 2

Also lost aboard

Rachael ReedCharmi FehrAnita Maria Cetinaand the two pilots

Rachael and Charmi were “full of joy and adventure.” Anita Maria cared for Emma and Charlie “with tenderness and devotion, and she was truly part of the family.” Our hearts are also with the families of the pilots.
